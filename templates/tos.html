How We Use Your Torn Data

YATA uses your Torn data to provide you with personalized features and functionality. This includes, but is not limited to, displaying your stats, tracking your progress, and providing recommendations based on your gameplay.

YATA may also use your data to improve the service, such as by analyzing usage patterns and identifying areas for improvement. This data will be used in aggregate form and will not be linked to your personal information.

Your API key may be used to update your player information, information regarding any faction or company you are in, and any other information that is available through the Torn API. This data is used to enhance your experience on YATA and provide you with the most accurate and up-to-date information possible.

Torn Data not available through public API calls is not shared with any other member without your consent. This includes, but is not limited to, your faction information, company information, and any other data that is not publicly available.

Your API key is stored securely and is not shared with any third parties. YATA takes the security of your data seriously and has implemented measures to protect it from unauthorized access or disclosure.
